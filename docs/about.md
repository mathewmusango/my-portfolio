## My Story

Hi, I'm a Platform Engineering Manager and Infrastructure Engineer based in Nairobi, Kenya, with more than 10 years of experience building secure, scalable, and highly available technology platforms.

### The Work

<div class="about-panel" markdown>

I believe the best infrastructure is almost invisible. When deployments are predictable, monitoring detects problems before users notice them, security is built into every layer, and recovery is continuously tested, engineering teams can spend their time creating products instead of managing complexity.

Throughout my career, I've designed and operated enterprise platforms supporting organizations across Africa, leading teams responsible for Kubernetes, cloud infrastructure, DevSecOps, automation, observability, enterprise networking, and security. My work has ranged from modernizing legacy infrastructure to building cloud-native platforms that improve developer productivity while meeting demanding security and compliance requirements, including PCI-DSS.

My passion lies in Platform Engineering—creating internal platforms that make software delivery faster, safer, and more reliable. I enjoy solving difficult infrastructure problems, automating repetitive work, and designing systems that continue performing as they grow from a handful of services to large, mission-critical environments.

</div>

### Outside the Keyboard

<div class="about-panel" markdown>

Outside of my day-to-day responsibilities, I'm constantly exploring emerging technologies. My current interests include AI agents, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), Model Context Protocol (MCP), AI-assisted operations (AIOps), and intelligent automation. I'm particularly interested in how artificial intelligence can improve platform reliability, observability, incident response, and developer experience.

When I'm away from the keyboard, I enjoy spending time with friends and family, exploring nature, and occasionally disappearing down the rabbit hole of a fascinating technical paper or open-source project.

</div>

<div class="about-philosophy" markdown>

**My Philosophy**

Build platforms that are secure, reliable, observable, and effortless to use — so developers can focus on creating great software instead of managing infrastructure.
