# Technical Expertise

Technology evolves rapidly, but the principles of building reliable platforms remain constant. Over the past 10+ years, I've designed, implemented, and operated enterprise infrastructure that enables engineering teams to build, deploy, and operate software securely and at scale.

My expertise spans Platform Engineering, Cloud Infrastructure, Kubernetes, DevSecOps, Observability, Enterprise Networking, Automation, and Engineering Leadership. Rather than focusing on individual tools, I focus on designing platforms that are secure by default, observable from day one, automated wherever possible, and resilient by design.
